Every menu changes over time. This report answers six simple questions about the Grizz Grill menu today, using real sales numbers and real costs — nothing guessed.

🏁 What Each Decision Means Operationally

Keep

No action needed. Continue ordering, prepping, and pricing exactly as today. These {{KEEP_COUNT}} items pass the 5-signal decision engine cleanly and are the operational backbone of the menu.

Refresh

Hand the recipe changes and cost deltas to the kitchen team for costing sign-off and a small test run before full rollout.
