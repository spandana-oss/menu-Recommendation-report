01 · About This Report

How These Recommendations Were Built

This is a new-menu development exercise, not a refresh of an existing menu — all items below are new introductions, generated for a restaurant that does not yet have a menu in this ZIP. Every claim in this report is labeled by type, so you always know whether you're looking at a fact, a calculation, or a judgment call.

D

Real Data

U.S. Census-style demographic, income, and commuter-flow figures for ZIP 38114, plus the actual list of 19 real restaurants operating in this ZIP.

C

Calculated Values

Numbers derived directly from the real data using a stated formula — household income-bracket shares, gross profit, food cost %, and profitability %.

I

Business Interpretation

Judgment calls that translate data into strategy — for example, reading "no cuisine-type field" restaurant names to infer a local style, or setting a price band.

E

Reasoned Estimates

Directional estimates built from real inputs where no single measured field exists — such as splitting a weekday vs. weekend customer mix using commuter data.

What data was used

ZIP-level population, income brackets, age, household size, employment and labor-force data; commuter inflow/outflow (who lives here vs. who works here); restaurant count and density; and the real names of the 19 existing restaurants in ZIP 38114.

What data was NOT used

This information is not available in the provided dataset and has not been estimated: customer reviews, star ratings, POS sales history, nutrition/calorie data, and a direct cuisine-type field for the comparable restaurants (their style is inferred from restaurant names only).

!

Methodology Philosophy

Comparable restaurants in this ZIP are used only to validate that a food style is already viable here — they are never the source of a recommendation. Every item is generated primarily from demographics, income-based customer segmentation, cuisine fit, market opportunity, and profitability. Click any card or metric throughout this report to see exactly how it was derived — evidence opens in a pop-up rather than pushing the page around.

Why isn't income broken out by ethnicity?

ZIP 38114 is 89.74% one race (Black/African American); every other group is under 5% of the population (Asian is roughly 36 people). At that sample size, the Census Bureau's own subgroup income estimates for a single ZIP carry a margin of error larger than the estimate itself, or are suppressed outright. Presenting a ZIP-level "median income by ethnicity" figure here would not be a real, defensible number — so it isn't shown. Read the full data-limitation note →
